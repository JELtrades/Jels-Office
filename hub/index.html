Time & Price

The only two tools that matter

Forget everything else. Time and price are the trade.

If you learn nothing else from JEL Trades, learn this: the market only moves on time and price. Not indicators. Not oscillators. Not a screen full of lines lagging behind what already happened. Time tells you when to look. Price tells you where it's going. Everything else is noise.

Price without time is just chasing — you'll see a perfect level at the wrong hour and get stopped out while being right. Time without price is just waiting with no plan — you'll be patient for a setup that never had a draw. Put them together and you stop guessing. You wait for the right time, at the right price, and only then do you act.
